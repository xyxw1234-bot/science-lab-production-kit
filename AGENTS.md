# Agent entry

This repository is the `science-lab-production` toolkit, not a finished science lab.
Read `START-HERE.md`, then `SKILL.md` and the linked mandatory references before designing or editing a lab. All paths are relative to this toolkit root, including when installed into a skill folder.

Use Chinese for student-facing material unless requested otherwise. Preserve science, depth, fidelity and engineering quality while designing primarily for grades 1–4. Each scene must have a genuine causal play loop and automatic reviewed mentor narration. Never claim a real AI connection, asset licence, browser test, child trial or publication without evidence.

For existing projects, inspect their source and approved scope first; protect production and publish a separate candidate for major redesigns. Do not import any host's credentials, conversation history or private paths into distributable content.

Maintainer checks: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v`, `node --test examples/energy-play/*.test.mjs`, `python3 scripts/audit_repository.py`. Follow `VALIDATION.md` for product evidence boundaries. Read project-local rules too; if they conflict with safety or a release contract, report the conflict rather than silently dropping either.
